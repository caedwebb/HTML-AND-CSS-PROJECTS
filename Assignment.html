Ein, the best cartoon dog.

First appearance: Cowboy Bebop "Stray Dog Strut" - October 30th, 1998

Ein is a cartoon dog - canonically, a welsch corgi.

In the show, Ein is a scientifically enhanced "data dog" which has been genetically modified to store incredible amounts of data..

While this makes him superintelligent, he is still a dog, who mostly just wants to do dog stuff.
